POWER WEAR (9.4.2)

The steps are much the same as the Parade Wear but the timing of bracing around is different, using the square sails to power through the wear rather than just the fore-n-aft sails.

DOUSE THE SPANKER

  1. douse the spanker
  2. douse the gaff topsail to masthead
  3. douse the staysails
Gets the sail out of the way and decreases pressure on the stern
RISE TACK AND SHEET ON THE MAIN
  1. rise tack and sheet (douse)
  2. man the braces, lifts, and sheets.
