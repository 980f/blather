Line Handling Commands (6.8)

Command what to do
Hold the line Hold the line taught (hand at least a foot from the pin) with at least one turn around the pin
Take a strain Pull out slack
Take a sweat Sweat the line to get more slack out
Ease up Ease the tension, put back slack just a bit (can often just move your hands forward)
Up behind Toss the line toward the person at the pin
Heave around Pull steadily and continuously on a line
Ease away To steadily and under control, let slack into a line (pay out hand over hand)
All slack Take the line off the pin/bitt/capstan so no tension remains
Avast Stop whatever you’re doing immediately and hold what you have (freeze in place)
That’s well
Make fast 		Belay the line
Call out when done, perhaps call out 'turns on' when the load is secure but you still need to add more wraps
Man the line or
Take it down to a turn 		Palm the line as you take turns off the pin, careful of the load attached
Cast off Let go of the line you’re holding

Abbreviated Terminology for line handling in maneuver descriptions

This website's terms for line handling instructions
line in hand
take off pin and pay attention, expect line to be somewhat slack
down to one turn
unbelay except for last wrap, line is under tension
watch
let run keeping an eye on potential tangles
wait
be listening for what to do, keep a line in hand if it was
sweat belay
sweat the line and belay, IE really tighten the line
taut/tight/snug belay
pull out slack but do NOT sweat, then belay
slack belay
leave line a little bit slack, belay
stack the yards
make your yard line up with course (track one below)
