Reef / Square two overhand knots, mirror imaged
  • gaskets on furled headsails
  • Jacob's ladder
  • finishing step of mousing (then add buttons)
Blood overhand but doubled before cinching tight
  • keep line from pulling out of block or fairlead
Figure 8 over-under-over and pull tight
  • ditto Blood but for gantline where it needs to be easier to untie
Sheet Bend wrap around bitter side of larger rope's bight first,
exit the loop over the bitter end of the bight
  • temporarily tie smaller line to heavier one to haul the heavier, such as up into rigging
Double Sheet Bend wrap twice before leading out and tightening
  • ditto Sheet Bend but when heavier line is slick or thick
Bowline bitter alternates over and under
double is tied in a bight including the starting loop.
  • mooring line
  • fix a line to a stanchion without it locking on to stanchion
  • tool lanyards, so that cord doesn't interfere as much with use
  • ??topgallant staysail sheet tied to bulwark
  • [double bowline for bosun chair]
Cow Hitch tuck ends through a bight after wrapping them around something
  • becket to jackstay
  • button for loose lines to shrouds/backstays
  • bitter end of shroud lanyards
  • [final step of gasket coil is a cow hitch]
Clove Hitch round turn and a half hitch
  • ratlines to shrouds
Constrictor bitter end over the standing line before finishing the clove
  • temporary whipping
  • heaving line to mooring line, make it slippery
Buntline clove on standing,
round the outside first so that as the loop shrinks it binds on the bitter end
  • lines to edges of sail
Round Turn and 2 Half Hitches round and loop,
clove on standing round the inside first
  • temporary hitch- such as small craft to a ring
Rolling Hitch clove with extra wraps before crossover and final half hitch
wrap towards load,last half hitch is furthest from load.
  • stopper such as on a halyard
Rolling Hitch to self tie to standing line, initial rounds to inside of loop
  • bitter end of gasket on furled fore-n-aft sails
  • as tent rope for awnings
Klemheist Hitch wrap from the Becket from center to each end,
loop that takes load tucks into the other
  • attach jigger tackle to a line

  • keep an otherwise loose line neat, esp. yardarm gasket

  • keep the pin from coming out

frap the wraps in different directions to squeeze them tight,
reef with buttons to finish
  • keep the load from slipping off of the hook
Marline Hitch constrictor, overhands around thing, constrictor
  • pull tighter on the bitter end of a line being tied
Marline Spike Hitch? loop forward, pick up standing line with spike and pull through loop
